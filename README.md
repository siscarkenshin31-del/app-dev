  # **Stranger Things**
  # **Description**
  >Stranger Things is a show about a group of kids in the 1980s who discover strange and creepy things happening in their small town. When one of their friends, Will, goes missing, they team up with a girl named Eleven who has special powers. Together, they uncover a secret world called the "Upside Down" and face terrifying monsters and government secrets. It’s a mix of friendship, mystery, and supernatural events.
